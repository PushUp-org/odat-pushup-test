Set-up for a fair test

  • Prop the phone side-on, about 2 m away, at floor level.
  • Keep your whole body in the picture, feet included. No rep counts while the legs are out of frame.
  • Good light from the front or side, not behind you.
  • Try slow, normal and fast reps, and a few knee push-ups and half reps: those should not count.
